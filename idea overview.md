# Kickoff Lab: Workshop Pack

Nov 23, 2026 · @phoneix

Kickoff Lab is a 60-minute workshop that teaches first-year EAIT students to be a better teammate from day one, by practising their team's first meeting twice: once cold, once with the tools.

## The problem

Teams don't fall apart in week 8. They fall apart in week 1, when the first meeting ends with "let's make a group chat" and nothing else. Most of us learned this the hard way in ENGG1100 and COMP1100: someone goes quiet, one person carries the project, internal deadlines never existed. Nobody teaches you how to start a team, or how to be a good teammate from the first conversation.

## At a glance

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Audience | First-year EAIT students about to start their first team-project course (ENGG1100, COMP1100, DECO1800) |
| Skill | Starting a team well: running and showing up to a first meeting so expectations are set from day one |
| Inner skills | Empathy (noticing what teammates are dealing with), emotional awareness (naming tension early), self mastery (owning your commitments) |
| Format | One 60-minute session, 30 people, 5 groups of 6 |
| Timing | O-Week or weeks 1 to 2, before teams form |
| Run by | 2 EAIT Ambassadors |

| Takeaway | A team charter template and a personal teammate pledge |

## SMART objectives

1. **Team:** by the end of the 60 minutes, each group's round 2 charter covers at least 8 of the 10 checklist items, up from its round 1 score.
2. **Individual:** in round 2, every participant makes at least 2 of the 3 good-teammate moves (shares their own constraint, draws in a quieter teammate, proposes a team rule), as tallied by their group's observer.
3. **Lasting:** every participant leaves with a written 3-line teammate pledge, and within 4 weeks at least half report that their real team used a charter or their pledge.

## How the hour runs

The core idea: do the first meeting cold, see what you missed, learn the tools, then do it again. Full timings and facilitator lines are in the Run sheet tab.

1. **Hook (5 min):** live poll on worst group project moments.
2. **Round 1 (15 min):** groups get a mock project and secret role cards, and run a 12-minute first meeting with no help. An observer tallies teammate moves.
3. **Reveal (10 min):** role cards read aloud; groups score their charter against the checklist.
4. **Teach (10 min):** the 5 questions every first meeting answers, and the 3 good-teammate moves.
5. **Round 2 (12 min):** new project, new role cards, same 12 minutes, now with the template.
6. **Score and pledge (8 min):** compare round 1 and 2, write a teammate pledge, fill in the exit card.

## How we'll know it worked

Three measures, all taken in the room except the last. Details are in the Measuring success tab.

- Charter checklist score, round 1 vs round 2 (team level)
- Observer tally of teammate moves, round 1 vs round 2 (individual level)
- Exit card confidence, before vs after, plus a 4-week follow-up form
