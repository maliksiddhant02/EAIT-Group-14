# Before the Group Chat

**EAIT Group 14 · UQ Inside Out Impact Challenge**

A 60-minute workshop that teaches first-year EAIT students to lead a group's first meeting. Students run a first meeting twice: once cold, then again with a one-page toolkit, a 30-minute gap between, and their score goes up in the room.

> Teams don't fail in week 8. They fail in week 1.

**New to the project?** Read `workshop/brief.md`, then click through the deck while reading `pitch/script.md`.

## Presenting the deck

1. Open `pitch/deck.html` in Chrome. Open it once while online so the Poppins font loads.
2. Press `F` for full screen.
3. Click, or press `→` or a presentation clicker, to advance. Every animation waits for a click. Press `←` to go back.
4. Add `#5.2` to the URL to jump to slide 5, click 2.
5. Add `?still` to the URL to show every build at once. This is the version to print to PDF if the organisers need a file.
